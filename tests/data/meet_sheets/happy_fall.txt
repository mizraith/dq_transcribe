                           PASA Happy Fall Y’all Block Party Meet
                                    October 11-12, 2025
PURPOSE:​          This sanctioned Block Party meet is for PASA swimmers at the C/B/A level. Times achieved will count
                   towards qualifying meets up through Far Westerns.
ENTRY FEE:​        $50 per swimmer. Please give payment to your coach with your entries by Wednesday, October 8.
                   Coaches, please submit entries via Hytek by 10 PM on Wednesday, October 8.
                   Each PASA site - Pay with one check made out to DZ SWIM, INC or via Zelled to swim4zapp@dzswim.com.
INVITED SITES:​    PASA Sites: DZ SWIM, DKS, LAGCC
LOCATION:​         Fletcher Middle School Swimming Pool 4277-4299 Terman Dr, Palo Alto, CA 94306: From Highway
                   280 take Page Mill East towards Palo Alto. Turn right on Foothill Expressway, turn left on Arastradero
                   Rd, make a right on Terman Dr and then make an immediate right. The pool will be on your left. From
                   Highway 101, take San Antonio Rd West, turn right on East Charleston Rd, continue on Arastradero
                   Rd, turn left on Terman Dr and make an immediate right. The pool will be on your left.
COURSE:​           Outdoor 25-yard heated pool with up to 8 competition lanes. One lane will be available for
                   warm-up/cool-down.​
TIME:​             The competition pool will be available for warm-ups each morning from 8:00 AM - 8:50 AM. The meet
                   will start at 9:00 AM. Competition should end by 1:00 PM.
CHECK IN:​         The meet will be pre-seeded. Swimmers should notify their coach if a situation arises in which you
                   cannot attend by the evening of Thursday, October 9.
VOLUNTEERS:​       Parents of swimmers will be asked to time at this meet.
OFFICIALS:​        All certified and trainee stroke & turn officials are welcome to help out. This meet will not count in
                   OTS nor will it count towards official certification, but your help would be very much appreciated.
FOOD:​             A limited snack bar will be available. Cash, Venmo, PayPal, Apple Cash, and Zelle will be accepted.
RULES:​            Current USA and Pacific Swimming rules will govern the meet. Pacific Swimming warm-up procedures
                   will be in effect.
EVENTS:​           All events are timed finals. 25 yard events will start on the non-block side of the pool. Swimmers may
                   compete in four (4) individual events per day with a maximum or two (2) 200 yard events per day.
